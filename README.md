# JackedIn

## Two Timelines. One Trap.
**In the new economy, you don't own your life. You lease it from the giants.**

---

### Season 1: The Digital Squeeze
**MAYA BROOKS** is a high-yield "Elite Asset," a ghost in the machine of global logistics. She spends her waking hours optimizing supply chains she will never touch, her every heartbeat and keystroke harvested by **ARIA**, a proprietary AI that predicts Maya’s desires before she feels them. Maya is a prisoner of the *Panopticon OS*, shackled by a $42,000 debt-load that grows faster than her efficiency rating.

The glitch in her programming arrives in the form of **MARCUS**, a relic of the analog age. A rugged, independent trucker who still believes in the weight of diesel and the truth of steel, Marcus represents a forbidden autonomy. Fueled by his defiance, Maya risks a terminal error: she begins repurposing her company’s own algorithmic weapons to forge a digital exit strategy. But in a world where "Inaction is Inefficiency," stepping out of line is a malfunction that the system is designed to purge.

---

### Season 2: The Physical Extraction (Prequel)
Two years prior, **MARCUS TURNER** was the "King of the Road"—a forty-year-old owner-operator with $25,000 in untraceable cash and a paid-off Peterbilt. He was a man of the tangible world, until the world stopped being tangible. As autonomous "Ghost Fleets" cannibalize the highways and his own children succumb to the predatory feedback loops of the digital void, Marcus makes a desperate play for relevance. He trades his freedom for the **Argus-5**, a state-of-the-art electric semi that promises a future but delivers a cage.

---

> *"The Grapes of Wrath for the Black Mirror generation."*

From the vertical, neon-drenched slums of Atlanta to the frozen, dark-fiber corridors of the Rockies, **JackedIn** is a cyber-noir odyssey about the commodification of human agency. It’s the story of a woman trying to hack a god, a man trying to outdrive the horizon, and the terrifying truth that when everything is connected, there is nowhere left to hide.
